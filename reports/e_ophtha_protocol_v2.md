# e-ophtha exploratory analysis protocol, version 2

Frozen before model inference. This version replaces the duplicate gate in `outputs/explainability/e_ophtha_review/protocol_frozen.md`; all its model, metric, threshold and mask rules remain in force. The former gate required a validated screen or adequate review before any inference. That standard has not been met: limited controls do not establish the real-world miss rate of either screening stage. At the user's request this is a revised externally sourced exploratory study, not independent validation. Historical checkpoint inputs, image-level and patient/eye-level independence remain unverified. The 6,980 lower-scoring pairs cannot be declared clear. No inference outcomes informed these decisions.

All 22 prioritized comparisons were reviewed at higher resolution, focusing on large vessels and the optic disc. None was confidently identified as the same image; all remain unresolved because of image quality, framing or possible transformations. This conservative, non-expert decision is not confirmation of correspondence or different patients. The 22 individually recorded decisions and reasons are in ignored `outputs/explainability/e_ophtha_review/decisions_v2.csv` (SHA-256 `e5588f0fe4ce168fd1ee047341a2188c43b0855f6523817f5e66f0b3328c4a85`) with corresponding numbered detail sheets. Preserve the original 7,002 pairs and screening outputs. No confirmed or plausible same-image match was assigned; if one had been identified it would have been excluded from every cohort. All 20 unique unresolved image hashes, including copies across subsets, are excluded from primary and evaluated separately in the unresolved sensitivity cohort; primary plus unresolved is a second sensitivity. This does not establish that primary images were unseen during training.

The frozen inventory and cohort decisions are `outputs/explainability/e_ophtha_review/cohort_v2.csv`, SHA-256 `5421005fcab4cda3c527618f4b04b7460703adb08b75a8e644bb637cc1fbbac0`. Primary counts: EX 44 positive and 33 source-negative, MA 143 positive and 221 source-negative. Unresolved: EX 3 positive and 2 source-negative, MA 5 positive and 12 source-negative. These include shared images in both applicable subset analyses and are not counts of independent people.

Use the existing EfficientNet-B0 checkpoint and preprocessing (224 square resize, tensor and ImageNet normalization), predicted-class Grad-CAM at `features[-1]`, ReLU and per-image min–max normalization; constant maps become zero. Bilinearly resize continuous CAM to native image dimensions using the existing evaluator and threshold at >=0.5. Sensitivity thresholds >=0.25 and >=0.75 apply to the primary nonzero mask rule. EX and MA positive masks are nonzero provisionally; MA sensitivities at 0.5 are exactly 255 and nonzero excluding 239 and 247. A positive becoming empty under a sensitivity rule is excluded from that positive metric with count, never relabeled negative. Masks are never interpolated. Positive metrics are pixel IoU, Dice and recorded-pixel coverage with unweighted mean and median; empty CAM with nonempty mask gives zeros. Source-negative cases are analyzed separately as thresholded activated fraction of the whole image rectangle and fraction with any activation. Negative status concerns the subset lesion only; no other lesion absence or healthy patient is implied.

Report separate EX and MA results, primary and unresolved sensitivity counts and all threshold/mask sensitivities. Do not pool subsets as independent observations or derive patient-level confidence intervals. Grades are not established for these images; predicted-class maps need not locate disease. MA labels can be points, and nonzero value semantics are not documented. These overlaps do not establish cause, clinical correctness or clinical validation.
