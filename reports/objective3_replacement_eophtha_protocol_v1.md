# Replacement model: e-ophtha evaluation protocol v1

Frozen on 2026-11-27 before training the replacement model. This is a separate protocol for one future replacement checkpoint; it does not alter the existing checkpoint or prior reports. Both e-ophtha subsets, including source-negative images, are reserved for evaluation only: no training, validation, class weighting, checkpoint selection, augmentation selection or hyperparameter selection uses their images, labels, masks or resulting scores. No replacement model inference is authorized before its training and model-selection choices are fixed and checkpoint hash recorded.

The original checkpoint has already been evaluated on these images and its results have been seen. This new protocol carries forward the already specified metrics and thresholds, but e-ophtha is not a pristine, previously uninspected test set. Earlier results and cohort decisions could influence study design. Findings will be exploratory, even with documented replacement training inputs; external sourcing does not establish patient- or image-level independence. No clinical validity is claimed.

## Method and reporting

Follow [the earlier e-ophtha protocol](e_ophtha_protocol_v2.md) for preprocessing and metrics, with only the checkpoint changed: 224×224 resize, ImageNet normalization, predicted-grade Grad-CAM at EfficientNet-B0 `features[-1]`, ReLU and per-image min–max normalization (constant maps become zero), bilinear mapping of the continuous map to native dimensions, and fixed threshold >=0.5. EX and MA are analyzed separately. Positive mask rule is nonzero provisionally, not an assertion of documented value meanings; no mask interpolation. Source-negative cases are only those expressly identified by the source for that subset; a missing mask is not a negative. Report per-image IoU, Dice, and coverage of recorded pixels, and unweighted means and medians; source-negatives instead get the fraction of the entire image rectangle activated and the fraction with any activation. Empty activation with nonempty mask produces zero overlap, and sensitivity masks that become empty are excluded with counts and identities.

Prespecified sensitivities: thresholds >=0.25 and >=0.75 with the primary mask; MA value 255 only and nonzero excluding 239/247 at threshold 0.5; the separately reported unresolved cohort and primary-plus-unresolved combined cohort. Do not choose or change a model, threshold, input preprocessing, or reporting outcome using these results. Report complete EX/MA results, exclusions and source-negative and source-positive results separately, along with code/checkpoint/manifests and environment hashes. Preserve source data, prior outputs and checkpoint. The existing evaluator hard-codes the old checkpoint and protocol hash, so a separately tested parameterized or replacement-specific evaluator is necessary before use; verify it loads the newly hashed checkpoint and reproduces the same metric definitions without overwriting existing results.

MA marks may be dots rather than complete lesion outlines; background outside annotations is not proven lesion-free. Whole-rectangle activation includes black borders. Unknown disease grades mean a predicted-class map is not necessarily a map for the reference disease class. Image and patient/eye independence remain unverified, even if the candidate screen excludes obvious matches. No patient-level confidence intervals, lesion-instance detection claim, causal explanation or clinical correctness claim follows from pixel overlap.
